Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 21, 2026

Thank you for playing JumpingSquare! We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our fast-paced, geometry-based game on Android and iOS devices.

Information Collection

JumpingSquare is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). You can enjoy the dynamic hill-generation, edge-sliding physics, and particle trails without worrying about us tracking your personal identity.

Local Storage & Game Data

To provide a seamless experience, we store certain data locally on your device using secure local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third parties. It includes:

Game Progress

High scores, gems, unlocked trails, and collected points during active gameplay.

Preferences

Sound toggles, UI settings, and other configurations adjusted in the Settings menu.

You can clear this data anytime using the "Reset Game Data" option in the game settings.

Third-Party Services

JumpingSquare does not use any third-party analytics or advertising networks. The game runs entirely offline, rendering beautiful graphics via React Native Skia and Reanimated without reporting your activity to external servers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our app's features or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top.
